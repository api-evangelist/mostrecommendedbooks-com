Most Recommended Books aggregates curated book recommendations, reading lists, series orders, and free summaries from experts and famous personalities. The platform offers a single place to discover, explore, and enjoy literature across genres, providing detailed lists, expert insights, and free summaries to help readers find their next great read.

**Areas:** Book · Recommendations · ReadingLists · Summaries · Literature · Company

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Most Recommended Books: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
